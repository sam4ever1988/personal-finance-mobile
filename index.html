How Import Works
• Imported rows are treated as statement transactions, not manual transactions.
• Before import, rows are compared with existing transactions on the same card. Matching date/amount/description rows are skipped automatically so reports do not double-count.
• A manual transaction can be reviewed against a matching imported statement transaction.
• Card payments/top-ups are treated as transfers and excluded from spending.
• You review the preview before importing.
